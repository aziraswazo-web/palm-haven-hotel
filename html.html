WELCOME TO PALM HAVEN

A Peaceful Place to Unwind

Experience Belizean Hospitality

Palm Haven Hotel is a modern boutique resort planned for Hopkins Village, Stann Creek District, Belize.

Inspired by palm trees, tropical plants, and the Caribbean Sea, our hotel offers comfortable accommodations and a relaxing atmosphere.

Whether you are traveling as a couple, with your family, or seeking adventure, Palm Haven Hotel welcomes you to experience the beauty and culture of Belize.
